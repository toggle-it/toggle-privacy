Privacy Policy for Toggle

Last updated: July 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Toggle ("Toggle," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Toggle mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Who we are

Toggle is a social habit-tracking application that lets you create personal habit toggles, participate in shared group tasks, and share activity with people you follow. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at lets.toggle.it@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to operate the App:

  • Account information: your email address and the username you choose when you sign up.
  • Profile information: your profile photo, if you choose to add one, and your selected app theme.
  • Content you create: the personal toggles and group tasks you make (including their names and any completion photos you upload), your streaks and completion history, and reactions you give to others' activity.
  • Social connections: the follow and mutual-friend relationships between you and other users, and the groups you join.

We do not collect your precise location, your contacts, or device identifiers for advertising. We do not use analytics or tracking tools that follow you across other apps or websites.

How we use your information

We use the information above only to provide and operate the App, including to:

  • create and manage your account and let you sign in;
  • display your toggles, tasks, streaks, and activity to you;
  • show your activity to people you have a follow or mutual relationship with, subject to your privacy settings;
  • enable group task rotation, completion, and verification among group members;
  • send account-related emails, such as email-change confirmations and password resets.

Your privacy controls

You control what others can see. You can mark any individual toggle or task as private, or make all of your toggles private or public at once. Items you mark private are not shown to other users — their content is excluded before it reaches anyone else's device. Public items may be visible to users who follow you or are mutual friends.

How your information is shared

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising or marketing purposes. Your information is shared only in these limited ways:

  • With other users: your username, profile photo, and public activity are visible to users you connect with, according to your privacy settings.
  • With our service provider: the App's data is stored and processed by our backend and hosting provider, which maintains our database and authentication on our behalf.
  • When required by law: we may disclose information if required to do so by law or valid legal process.

Data storage and security

Your data is stored on managed database infrastructure provided by our backend provider. Access to your own data is protected by account authentication and database access controls. While no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Deleting your account and data

You can permanently delete your account at any time from within the App, in the account settings ("Danger Zone") section. Deleting your account removes your profile, toggles, tasks, completions, reactions, follow relationships, and associated content. This action is permanent and cannot be undone. If you need help deleting your account, contact us at lets.toggle.it@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

Toggle is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact us

If you have questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, contact us at lets.toggle.it@gmail.com.
