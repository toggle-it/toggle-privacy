Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

Toggle is a social habit-tracking app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions: lets.toggle.it@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account: your email address and username.
  • Profile: an optional profile photo and your app theme.
  • Your activity: habits (toggles), groups and group tasks, completion photos you upload, streaks, history, reactions (boosts) and nudges.
  • Toggle Packs: packs you create (name, emoji, description, habits) and the packs you join.
  • Connections: who you follow, your mutuals, groups you join, and users you block.
  • Reports: if you report content, the report reason, any details you write, and what was reported.
  • Notifications: if you turn on push notifications, a device token from Apple and your time zone, so we can deliver notifications at sensible times.
  • Beta sign-up: if you sign up on our website for the beta, your email address, which flyer or link you came from, and a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address used only to stop spam.

We do not collect precise location, contacts or advertising identifiers, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To run your account and sign you in.
  • To show your activity to the people you've allowed to see it.
  • To run groups, task rotation, Toggle Packs and contests.
  • To send push notifications you've turned on (boosts, nudges, new mutuals, streak reminders, "your turn"). You can turn each type off in the app or in iPhone Settings.
  • To review reports and keep Toggle safe.
  • To send account emails, such as password resets, and, if you signed up for the beta, the TestFlight invite.

Privacy controls

Every habit can be public or private. Private habits never appear to other users. You can block anyone; blocked users can't see your activity or packs, and you won't see theirs.

Sharing

We don't sell your information or share it for advertising. We share it only with other users as your settings allow; with service providers that run Toggle for us (Supabase for our database and servers, Apple for push notifications, Google Gmail for sending emails); and when the law requires it.

Storage and security

Your data is stored with Supabase and protected by sign-in and database access rules. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Keeping and deleting data

You can permanently delete your account in the app (Profile → Account settings → Danger Zone). This removes your profile, activity and connections and can't be undone. Beta sign-up emails are kept until the beta ends or you ask us to remove yours.

Children

Toggle is not meant for children under 13. If we learn we've collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it.

Changes

We'll update the date above when this policy changes.
