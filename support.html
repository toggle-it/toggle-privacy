Common questions

How do I report or block someone? Tap ⋯ on their post or profile, then choose Report or Block. Blocked accounts are listed in the sidebar under Blocked accounts.

How do I join a Toggle Pack? Open the Packs tab and pick one from Popular, or type a pack code a friend sent you.

A habit didn't reset. Habits reset when their timer runs out. Open the app and it will catch up. Still stuck? Email us.

How do I delete my account? Profile → Account settings → Danger Zone → Delete account. This can't be undone.

Notifications aren't coming through. Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Toggle, and the Notifications page in the app's sidebar.
